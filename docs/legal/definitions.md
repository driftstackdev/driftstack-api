# Driftstack — defined terms (shared)

**Version:** 1.1 · **Effective:** 2026-07-17

This file is the source of truth for terminology used across the
Driftstack legal document set: the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md),
the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), the [Data Processing Agreement](dpa.md),
and the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use-policy.md). When any of
those documents capitalises a term, this file controls.

If a term needs different meanings in different documents, it is
renamed in one of them. We do not redefine.

## Parties + entities

**"Driftstack"**, **"we"**, **"us"**, or **"our"** means Driftstack B.V., a private limited company (_besloten vennootschap_) organised under the laws of the Netherlands, established in Amsterdam.

**"Customer"** or **"you"** means the legal entity that has accepted
the Terms of Service and on whose behalf the Service is used, together
with that entity's Authorized Users.

**"Authorized User"** means an individual employee, contractor, or
agent of Customer that Customer has granted access to the Service. An
Authorized User acts on Customer's behalf and binds Customer.

**"Party"** means Driftstack or Customer; **"Parties"** means both.

## Service + product terms

**"Service"** or **"Platform"** means the Driftstack iPhone Safari
automation platform, including the API, the self-hosted GUI Client,
the SDKs published on public package registries (npm, PyPI, Go modules),
and the mac mini fleet that hosts the underlying WebKit driver
infrastructure.

**"API"** means the HTTP application programming interface published
under the `/v1/` path at
[https://api.driftstack.dev](https://api.driftstack.dev). Its current
developer reference is published at
[https://docs.driftstack.dev/api/](https://docs.driftstack.dev/api/).

**"API Key"** means an authentication credential issued to Customer by
Driftstack that authorises calls to the API on Customer's behalf. API
Keys are revocable and limited by the scope taxonomy published in the
current API reference.

**"GUI Client"** means the Tauri-based desktop application distributed
by Driftstack that connects to Driftstack Cloud or to a compatible
self-hosted Driftstack Node selected by Customer.

**"SDK"** or **"SDKs"** means the official client libraries published
by Driftstack: `@driftstack/sdk` (TypeScript, npm), `driftstack-sdk`
(Python, PyPI), and the Go SDK at
`github.com/driftstackdev/driftstack-api/packages/sdk-go`.

**"Session"** means an instance of an iPhone-archetype Safari runtime
provisioned on Driftstack's mac mini fleet under Customer's account,
identified by a `ses_<uuid>` identifier through the API.

**"Recording"** means a Customer-controlled desktop-local NDJSON
archive of streamed frames captured by the GUI Client during a
Session. Recordings exist only when Customer explicitly enables
recording and remain on Customer's device; the recording workflow
does not upload them to Driftstack.

**"Capture"** means a one-shot screenshot, DOM snapshot, or PDF
generated by the API's `capture` endpoint during a Session, returned
inline to Customer and not retained by that endpoint.

**"Customer Workflow"** means the automation logic, recipes, scripts,
or instructions Customer provides to the Service to drive Sessions.

**"Subscription"** means Customer's ongoing access to the Service
under a tier and price published on Driftstack's current pricing page
or agreed in an Order Form. The applicable checkout or Order Form is
the commercial source of truth for Customer's price and payment rail.

## Data terms (GDPR-aligned)

**"Personal Data"** has the meaning in Article 4(1) GDPR: any
information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

**"Special Category Data"** has the meaning in Article 9 GDPR:
personal data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions,
religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic
data, biometric data uniquely identifying a person, data concerning
health, or data concerning a person's sex life or sexual orientation.

**"Data Subject"** has the meaning in Article 4(1) GDPR: the
identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data
relates.

**"Processing"** has the meaning in Article 4(2) GDPR: any operation
performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage,
transmission, alteration, retrieval, erasure, or destruction.

**"Controller"** has the meaning in Article 4(7) GDPR: the natural or
legal person that determines the purposes and means of Processing.

**"Processor"** has the meaning in Article 4(8) GDPR: the natural or
legal person that Processes Personal Data on behalf of a Controller.

**"Sub-processor"** means a third-party Processor engaged by
Driftstack to Process Personal Data on Customer's behalf in the
course of providing the Service. The current Sub-processor list is
maintained in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md#sub-processors)
and the [DPA Annex 3](dpa.md#annex-3-sub-processors).

**"Customer-Connected Service"** means a third-party service that
Customer integrates with the Service using Customer's own
credentials, Customer's own account, and Customer's own contractual
relationship with that third party. Customer-Connected Services
include (without limitation) HTTP/SOCKS5 proxies, captcha-solving
services (e.g. 2Captcha, CapSolver), email services accessed by
Customer's IMAP or OAuth credentials, and SMS verification services
(e.g. TextVerified, Twilio).

> **Customer-Connected Services are not Sub-processors.** Driftstack
> does not contract with the third-party provider, does not receive
> service from them, and does not Process data on Driftstack's
> behalf through them. The third-party Processes data on **Customer's**
> instruction via **Customer's** credentials. The DPA codifies this
> distinction.

**"Customer Data"** means all data, content, and information that
Customer or its Authorized Users submit or transmit to the Service,
including Customer Workflows, Session inputs, live-session media,
Capture requests, and any Personal Data Customer chooses to process
through the Service. A Recording that remains solely on Customer's
device is not submitted to Driftstack by the recording workflow.

**"Customer-Provided Secrets"** means the credentials Customer
supplies for use in Sessions, including (without limitation) proxy
authentication credentials, captcha-service API keys, email
credentials, and SMS-service API keys. Customer-Provided Secrets are
a subset of Customer Data with elevated handling obligations
documented in the DPA and the Privacy Policy.

**"Account Data"** means information identifying Customer or its
Authorized Users for the purpose of administering the contractual
relationship, including legal entity name, billing address, VAT/BTW
number, named contacts, billing email, and authentication
identifiers.

**"Session Metadata"** means non-content information about a Session,
including session id, customer id, target site identity, timestamps,
duration, success/failure, and aggregate counters used for capacity
planning.

## Compliance + transfer terms

**"GDPR"** means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament
and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural
persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the
free movement of such data.

**"AVG"** means the _Algemene verordening gegevensbescherming_, the
Dutch implementing instrument of the GDPR. Where this document
references the GDPR, equivalent provisions of the AVG apply where
Dutch law is the controlling law of the relationship.

**"AP"** means the Dutch Data Protection Authority (_Autoriteit
Persoonsgegevens_), the supervisory authority of the Netherlands
under Article 51 GDPR.

**"SCCs"** means the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by
Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021 (the
"2021 SCCs"), incorporated by reference where Personal Data is
transferred to a third country lacking an adequacy decision under
Article 45 GDPR.

**"DPF"** means the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adopted by
Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023, and
its self-certification mechanism for US-based recipients of EU
Personal Data.

**"Adequate Country"** means a country, territory, sector, or
international organisation that the European Commission has
determined provides an adequate level of protection for Personal Data
under Article 45 GDPR.

**"DPA"** in this document set means the Data Processing Agreement at
[`docs/legal/dpa.md`](dpa.md). Where capitalised in a sentence
discussing a regulator (e.g. "the Dutch DPA"), DPA refers to the data
protection authority and the context disambiguates.

## Commercial + financial terms

**"Fees"** means the charges payable by Customer for the Service under
Customer's Subscription, current checkout, or Order Form, plus
applicable taxes. Standard bundled-LLM turns currently post an
included-service accounting value against Customer's enforced budget;
that value is not a separately itemised Stripe charge.

the Dutch implementation of EU VAT under Directive 2006/112/EC.

**"Reverse-Charge VAT"** means the EU VAT mechanism under which the
Customer (a B2B recipient established in an EU member state other than
the Netherlands and holding a valid VAT identification number) accounts
for VAT in their own member state, and Driftstack invoices without
Dutch BTW. The Customer is responsible for verifying its own VAT
status.

**"Stripe"** means Stripe Payments Europe, Limited
(Ireland-incorporated entity with company number 538590) and its
affiliates, the payment processor for fiat (card, SEPA, iDEAL,
Bancontact) Subscription Fees from Customers established in the
European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland; and
Stripe, Inc. (Delaware, USA) for Customers established outside the
EEA / UK / CH.

**"Moneybird"** means Moneybird B.V. (Dutch entity, Utrecht), the
accounting and invoicing platform used to generate VAT-compliant
invoices for Subscription Fees.

**"MacStadium"** means MacStadium, Inc. (Delaware, USA), the data

**"Hetzner"** means Hetzner Online GmbH (Gunzenhausen, Germany), the
provider of the Cloud VM hosting Driftstack's control plane, admin
panel, and onboarding API surface.

**"Neon"** means Neon, Inc. (Delaware, USA), the managed Postgres

**"Upstash"** means Upstash, Inc. (Delaware, USA), the managed Redis
provider for caches, rate-limit buckets, and ephemeral session state.
Data residency is the EU Frankfurt region.

**"Cloudflare"** means Cloudflare, Inc. (Delaware, USA), the provider

hosting, and R2 object storage for customer-uploaded profile avatars,

**"Postmark"** means the transactional email service operated by

**"Sentry"** means the error tracking and performance monitoring

**"Anthropic"** means Anthropic, PBC (Delaware, USA), the provider of

## Documents + acceptance

**"Terms of Service"** or **"ToS"** means [`docs/legal/terms-of-service.md`](terms-of-service.md).

**"Privacy Policy"** means [`docs/legal/privacy-policy.md`](privacy-policy.md).

**"AUP"** or **"Acceptable Use Policy"** means [`docs/legal/acceptable-use-policy.md`](acceptable-use-policy.md).

**"Effective Date"** means the date displayed in the document header.
For a given Customer, the Effective Date with respect to that
Customer is the later of (a) the document's published Effective Date
or (b) the timestamp of Customer's most recent recorded acceptance of
that document version.

**"Acceptance"** means a Customer-attributed event recorded by the API
endpoint `POST /v1/legal/accept` carrying `account_id`, `document_key`,
`version`, `content_hash`, and `accepted_at`. A Customer's continued
use of the Service after a Major version bump (without recording a
new Acceptance) constitutes objection to the new version and entitles
Customer to terminate the Subscription on notice without penalty.

**"Material Change"** means a change to a Document that materially
alters Customer's rights, obligations, or fees; adds or removes a
Sub-processor; or changes the Service's offered jurisdictions. Material
Changes are released as Major version bumps and require a new
Acceptance.

## Default conventions

When a document references a duration without specifying a basis,
the duration runs from the event being referenced (e.g. "30 days" in
the context of a sub-processor objection runs from the date Driftstack
notifies Customer of the new sub-processor).

When a document references "written notice", written includes
electronic notice (email, in-product banner, API response surface) and
notice posted at the public URL of the document. Postal mail is
sufficient but not required.

When a document is silent on an obligation, the GDPR / AVG / Dutch
Civil Code default applies to the Driftstack-Customer relationship.

When a Customer's national law mandates a more protective regime than
this document set provides, the more protective regime controls for
that Customer.
