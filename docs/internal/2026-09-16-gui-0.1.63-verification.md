# gui-v0.1.63 — verification log (runbook §"verify each claim on the running artifact")

Every claim the notes make, the observable that settles it, and how it was produced.
Claims are listed BEFORE the notes were written, not after.

## 1. "The status bar names what it is waiting for"

- Owner's words: "keeps stauts 'connecting' only opens first page, then cant do anything";
  "not worked, couldnt show the live eview - connected, but no video arrived".
- Observable: the text in the toolbar pill on a session that is not yet live, and
  whether the profile/device name survives beside it.
- Produced: rendered the real `DeviceToolbar` in the visual harness under Chromium at
  the REAL 402px default width and at `SIM_MIN_WIDTH` (280px), screenshotted, and looked.
- Result: at 402px — "amsterdam … · iPho… ● WAITING FOR THE SCREEN…", profile and device
  both present. At 280px the names give way and the blocker survives.
- ⛔ Attacked once: the first pass ran at 288px and showed the profile name crushed to
  the single letter "a". That is NOT an artefact of testing too narrow — SIM_MIN_WIDTH
  is 280, which a customer can drag to. Fixed (own truncating span, non-shrinking dot),
  re-rendered, re-looked. A max-width was tried and REMOVED: it truncated the label at
  the default width and did not recover the name at the minimum.
- VERIFIED.

## 6. "A refusal that was ours stopped being blamed on your proxy"

- Observable: the sentence a customer reads when a session is refused because its
  traffic could not be confirmed to be leaving through their proxy, and whether it
  is marked as theirs to act on.
- Produced: the real `AgentSessionPanel` rendered for the new code, overlay text
  read from the DOM. Mutation-proved TWICE — moving the branch below the
  `/^(proxy_|egress_)/` prefix reds two arms (the prefix would otherwise swallow a
  token that starts with `egress_`), and a CONTROL asserts a genuine proxy failure
  still says so and still points at the customer.
- ⛔ Attacked once, and it changed the copy: the first draft ended "starting it
  again usually works". The path is already wrapped in transient retry, so every
  refusal observed on the fleet had ALREADY failed every automatic attempt. That
  sentence would have been a second falsehood told to someone who had retried and
  failed, stacked on the first. Removed.
- ⚠️ And one over-claim caught in the NOTES rather than the code: they read "it
  often wasn't yours". Nobody measured that distribution — six refusals observed,
  one examined. Now "it may not be yours", which is what the evidence supports.
- VERIFIED.

## 7. "The Proxies tab fits your window — no more scrolling sideways, and a row opens to show everything"

- Owner's words (2026-09-17): "it has too many rows and its not very clean to move to need to go
  to left and right to see informatino, this needs better architecutre to more cleanly view and
  match screen. But still without removing useful information."
- Observable: horizontal overflow of the table's own scroll box; the height of every row; whether
  the endpoint's port stays inside its cell; what the opened detail row contains.
- Produced: the REAL `ProxiesView` mounted in the visual harness (`?scene=audit-proxies`, seven
  fixture proxies: healthy, 5-hours-old readings, failed with a long sentence, WireGuard up,
  OpenVPN untested, HTTP, and a 60-character host) under Chromium at a TRUE 960px and 1280px
  window (`&stage=WxH` lays the app out at that width — a scaled 1280 stage is not a 960 render),
  dark and light, screenshotted, and looked.
- Result: no horizontal overflow at either width. Rows 70–76px at 1280 (103 for the failed row's
  two-line sentence); 72–126px at 960. Port visible on 7 of 7 rows including the 60-character
  host. The detail row shows the full endpoint, exit network and timezone, both latencies with
  when they were taken, every capability as a sentence, the device-stack reading explained, and
  the full failure sentence.
- ⛔ Attacked twice. (1) The first cut PASSED every measurement — nothing overflowed, nothing left
  its cell, two reviewers agreed — and looked wrong: four capability chips stacked one per line
  beside a Health column with a visible empty gap, rows 110–140px. Only opening the PNG showed it.
  Column widths rebalanced, re-rendered, re-looked. (2) A truncating `host:port` ate the PORT
  first ("…example.com:10…"); the host truncates now and the port never does.
- Also: mounting this view in the audit brought it under the text-quality gate, which it FAILED
  with 32 findings (8px labels; "not tested" at 3.0:1). Fixed; the gate reports 0 findings across
  32 scene×theme cells locally AND on Linux CI for `4d82f3bad`, and its positive control still

- VERIFIED (in the harness; see the unverifiable list for the packaged app).

  maintainer's box; the release's own asset checks (10 assets, 9 platform keys, 0 unsigned)
  are the substitute and are not the same thing.

- **A real Test storing its QUIC / UDP reading on production** (claim 9's write). It needs an

  without testing there.
- **The automatic check of missing readings, running inside the packaged app.** Its planner,
  budget (3 rows / 1 tunnel per 15 minutes), 6-hour backoff, the no-upload rule and the
  "never beside a sweep" latch are each proven by mutation in tests, and four independent
  reviewers confirmed no automatic path can upload credentials — but nobody has watched a packaged
  build do it against a live account. The notes may say the app now does this; they may NOT say
  it was observed. Owner-side observable: a saved proxy showing "not measured" chips gains them
  within ~15 minutes of opening the Proxies tab, with no click.
